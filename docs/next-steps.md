# Ratan Galaxy sample site: next steps

Written 10 November 2026. In priority order: the first block is housekeeping that should happen in the first week, the second is what the site needs before anyone outside the team sees it, the third is product work worth doing, the fourth is the long tail. Effort is a rough size for one person who knows the stack: S is a day or less, M is up to a week, L is more.

## A. First week

1. **Settle the uncommitted overview redesign (S).** The working tree holds a redesign of the home stage (isometric dusk view on a slab, selected-unit card, launch button, a dock of controls) left by a side session that was still running on 10 November; see section 2 of `docs/handover.md`. Open the home page, try the tour, the walk, the presets, the slider, the filters, click a home and the gate, at 1440 and 375, then either commit it with a prose message or discard it. Also decide whether `blender/export_glb.py`, `render_overview.py` and `make_overview.py` stay (they should: they regenerate the glb and the fallback).
2. **Push or transfer the repository (S).** Three commits are local only. The remote is the author's personal GitHub account; fork it into the company's organisation or transfer it, then push. Before pushing, confirm `docs/pictures/` is still untracked (`git status`); it contains copyrighted reference material and must never reach a public remote.
3. **Correct the stale line in the facts sheet (S).** `docs/ratan-galaxy-facts.md`, under "The Phase II brochure's floor plans", still says Block A's plans are a follow-up. They were applied in commit `19f8fe2`. Remove the sentence.
4. **Remove the superseded stills (S).** `assets/ratan-galaxy-day*.jpg` and `-dusk*.jpg` (the three.js snapshots) are no longer referenced once the Blender renders are in; delete them and the `credits.json` rows that point at them. Check with a grep across `src/` first.
5. **Clean `data/db.json` (S).** It holds test bookings from the first day, some naming the earlier fictional projects. Delete the file; the server recreates it.
6. **Replace the development tooling that lived outside the repo (M).** The lint, the 375-route check and the screenshot runs were scripts in the author's Claude plugin folder and scratchpads (section 8 of the hand-over). Recreate the two that matter as repository scripts under `tools/` with Playwright or Puppeteer: a route check that loads every page at 375 and fails on horizontal overflow, naming the element; and a screenshot run at 1440 and 375. Add them to `package.json` scripts.

## B. Before anyone outside sees it

7. **Decide what the site is for (decision, not code).** It was built as a showcase of what a real-estate site can do, around a real society, by someone who lives there. Three honest futures: keep it as the company's demo (then the sample labels and the imprint stay and nothing else is needed); make it the society's own site for its owners' association (then the builder and the RWA should be asked for official photographs, current resale guidance and permission to use the name, and the "sales lounge" becomes the RWA office); or turn it into a template for paying developers (then the Ratan Galaxy content becomes one example dataset). Most of the items below depend on this choice.
8. **Hosting and HTTPS (S to M).** The server is a single Node process serving static files and a JSON store. The simplest deployment is one small VM or container behind a reverse proxy that terminates TLS, with `ADMIN_PASSWORD` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` as environment secrets and `data/` on a persistent volume. Basic auth on `/admin/` is only acceptable over HTTPS.
9. **A real store (M).** Replace `lib/store.mjs` (one JSON file rewritten on every change, one process) with SQLite through `node:sqlite` or `better-sqlite3`, keeping the same functions (`load`, `mutate`, `nextRef`, `slotsLeft`) so `api.mjs` and `pages.mjs` do not change. Add a nightly dump.
10. **Email and SMS for bookings (M).** Nothing is sent today. A booking should confirm to the visitor and notify the desk; a cancellation likewise. Pick one transactional provider and add a `lib/notify.mjs` called from `createBooking`, `cancelBooking` and `setBookingStatus`. Keep the site working when the provider is down.
11. **Run the furniture scan against the live API once (S).** `lib/scan.mjs` has only been exercised on its 400 and 503 paths. With a key, scan five real photographs, check the JSON shape and the size estimates, and tune the prompt if the sizes are off. Set a monthly spend cap on the key; scans are rate-limited to 30 an hour per address but not per month.
12. **Accessibility pass (M).** The 3D stages are `aria-hidden` and every control is a real button with a label, but nobody has run the site through a screen reader or keyboard-only. Check focus order through the hero dock and the flat panel, the colour contrast of `--on-dark-muted` on the board, and that the tour's captions are announced. Add a visible skip link.
13. **Performance budget on a real phone (M).** The headless numbers (home about 59 fps at 1440 with the post pass; phone about 29) came from a desktop GPU emulating a phone viewport. Measure on a mid-range Android: the glb load, first frame, and the furniture viewer with six pieces placed. Likely wins: a smaller Draco glb for phones, lazy-loading the furniture catalogue's thumbnails, and self-hosting the fonts as woff2 subsets.
14. **Own the CDN dependencies (S).** three.js, the Draco decoder and both type families come from third-party CDNs at runtime. Vendor three.js 0.170 and the Draco decoder into `src/static/vendor/` and self-host the fonts, so the site does not change when a CDN does.

## C. Product work worth doing

15. **Photographs of the society (S, but blocked on the author).** The site has none; the renders stand in. Ask the departing author, or any resident, for a dozen phone photographs (the gate, each block from the lawn, the pool, the club, a corridor, a balcony view) and put them in the home gallery, the towers hero and the amenities row with a simple credit. The renders then become the model's own pictures rather than stand-ins.
16. **The flat viewer as the sales tool (M).** It already walks, furnishes and scans. Add a "share this flat" link that encodes the furniture layout in the URL (it is in `localStorage` per plan today), a measuring tape between two clicks, and a sun-path slider so a buyer sees the morning light in a bedroom. Each is a day.
17. **Resale listings (M).** Nothing on the pricing page is a live listing. If the site serves the RWA or an agent, add a small listings table (block, floor, flat, asking price, contact) edited from the admin desk, and let the hero paint the flats that are for sale.
18. **A real map (S).** The cream sketch map is deliberately a drawing. For directions a buyer actually follows, add a "Open in Google Maps" link on the location page with the plot's coordinates, and keep the sketch for the page.
19. **The six plan pages (S).** The plan cards are generated from `plans.js`; a page per plan with the room table, the balconies and the flats that carry it is a short template away and would be the pages a buyer searches for.
20. **Block A's facade from drawings (L).** The Blender model is built from photographs read on the portals. If the builder or the RWA can supply the elevation drawings, rebuild `step_block` in `build_site.py` from them; the glb and the renders regenerate from the script.

## D. The long tail

21. Tests. There are none. Start with the API (`api.mjs` validation, slot capacity, cancellation) using `node:test`, then a smoke test that builds the site and asserts every page renders with its title.
22. Analytics, if the site goes public: a privacy-respecting counter, no cookies, so the privacy page stays true.
23. The `price-sheet.html` print stylesheet was checked in Chrome only; check Safari and Firefox print output.
24. The open question from 7 November about seven three.js-ecosystem repositories the author found on social media was never answered; none was adopted. Nothing to do unless someone wants them.
25. The old "notice board" and "The place carries it" directions live only in git history. If a design review ever asks for the comparison, `git show 57d6acc:src/css/site.css` has the board and `7532060` the first direction.

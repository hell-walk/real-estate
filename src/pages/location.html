Vrindavan Yojna, the housing scheme south of Shaheed Path between Sultanpur Road and the airport road. Pin code 226029.

Where you are going, and how far

Leave Shaheed Path at the Vrindavan Yojna turn, two to three kilometres from the gate, and follow the sector roads south. Lucknow Public School is about a kilometre before the gate on the left. The main gate is on the south road of plots GH-01 and GH-02.

From Sultanpur Road it is about 5.7 km; from Gomti Nagar Extension about 6 km. The builder’s brochure puts the airport, SGPGI and the railway within six kilometres and a commercial market and community centre next to the plot. The map is a sketch; the lounge sends a pin with the booking.

This side of the city

South Lucknow is where the big new things went: Ekana cricket stadium, Janeshwar Mishra Park with its lake, the two malls on Shaheed Path, the hospitals on the Raebareli road. Vrindavan Yojna sits in the middle of them, a planned scheme with sector roads, which is why the ground here is flat and the roads are wide.

The old city, with the Imambaras and Hazratganj, is a 35 minute drive the other way across the Gomti.

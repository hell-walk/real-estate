The ridge road,
past Bavdhan

3.8 km of tarred road climbing from the Bavdhan junction to our gate, 420 metres above the city.

Where you are going

Leave the Paud road at the Bavdhan junction, pass the hospital on your left, and the ridge road begins at the water tank. It climbs in four bends, lit the whole way, and ends at the gate. There is nothing else on it.

From Baner it is twenty minutes; from Hinjawadi eighteen by the bypass, with no city traffic in between. The map is a sketch; the lounge will send you a pin.

The road, in order

  1. 1962

    A cart track to the plateau farms, used by the mango growers and, in May, by everyone else.

  2. 2004

    Tarred for the first time by the district, one lane, with the bends that are still there.

  3. 2022

    Rebuilt by us at 7 m wide with a storm drain on the hill side and lighting all the way, handed back to the district on completion.

  4. 2026

    Three monsoons without a closed day. The water tank at the bottom is where the school buses turn.

What a visit looks like

  1. Before

    Open the flat you want to see in the 3D viewer and note the block, floor and number. Nidhi calls the day before to confirm the slot and sends a pin for the gate.

  2. At the gate

    Fifteen minutes in the lounge with the master plan, the floor plans and the chart of what is for sale. Bring the people who will decide; it saves a second trip.

  3. In the block

    The lift to the floor, the flat itself if the owner has agreed, and a look at the one above or below if not. Then the club, the pool and the track. About an hour.

  4. After

    The plan set and the price sheet for the homes you liked, by email the same evening. Nobody calls you until you call us, unless you asked us to.

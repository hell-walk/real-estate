What four acres is for

Seven in ten stay open

Three blocks on 16,851 square metres, and about seventy per cent of it left as lawn, garden, courts and the pool. The blocks stand in a row along the plot, so every balcony looks over ground, not at the next tower.

Built in two phases, both done

Blocks B and C went up first, basement, ground and nine floors, and were handed over in June 2018. Block A followed with nine floors over eleven shops on the ground floor, finished in November 2024. Nothing here is a promise about the future.

A club at the east end

The round clubhouse with its hall and gym, the swimming pool and the kids' pool, the sand pit and the amphitheatre steps sit together at the east end of the plot. The tennis and basketball courts are at the west end, past Block C.

The hospital is ten minutes

SGPGI and Medanta are three to four kilometres away, Lucknow Public School about one, and Lulu Mall and Phoenix Palassio are on Shaheed Path within five. The airport is about eleven kilometres by the same road.

Is Ratan Galaxy RERA registered?

Yes, in two phases. Phase I, Blocks B and C, is UPRERAPRJ8949. Phase II, Block A, is UPRERAPRJ718664. The promoter is Ratan Housing Development Limited, UPRERAPRM15031.

Is it ready to move in?

Yes. Blocks B and C were completed on 30 June 2018 and Block A on 13 November 2024. What is on sale now is resale from owners, and the shops in Block A.

Which flats are which on a floor?

In Blocks A and C each floor has fourteen homes: two four bedroom at the ends of the X, eight three bedroom and four two bedroom. Block B is all two bedroom. The 3D viewer opens any of them by block, floor and number.

Can I bring my own bank for the loan?

The listings name Axis, Bank of Baroda, HDFC, ICICI and Punjab National Bank as approved. Others will want the usual legal check on a resale.

What are the monthly maintenance charges?

A sample figure of ₹2.50 per sq ft a month is used on this site, which covers the club, security, the lifts, the pool and the gardens. The society's actual charge is set by its owners' association.

Is this the builder's website?

No. This is an unofficial sample site. The blocks, floors and room sizes follow the public RERA records and the builder's published plans; the prices, the people and the lounge are invented and labelled as samples.

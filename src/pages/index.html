What fourteen acres is for

Seven in ten acres stay open

Six blocks of eleven floors on 29% of the land. The rest is the stream, the old mango grove, two lawns and the walking loop of 1.4 km along the ridge edge.

Built from the hill itself

The basalt cut for the foundations is dressed on site and comes back as the boundary wall, the clubhouse plinth and the stream steps. Nothing is carted away.

A club with a view, not a lobby

A 24,000 sq ft clubhouse on the western edge: a 25 m lap pool, a gym that opens onto the lawn, a library, two squash courts and a terrace for the monsoon.

The school run is twelve minutes

Three schools within 4 km, a hospital with an emergency ward at 6 km, and the Hinjawadi IT parks at 18 minutes by the ridge road, with no city traffic in between.

Is Verdant Ridge RERA registered?

Yes. The project carries a Maharashtra RERA registration; on this sample site the number is a placeholder and the certificate is shown at the lounge.

What is the possession date, and what if it slips?

December 2028 for blocks A to D, June 2029 for E and F. The agreement sets a penalty of ₹14 per sq ft a month to you after a six month grace period.

How is the ridge road in the monsoon?

Tarred and lit for the full 3.8 km since 2022, with a storm drain on the hill side. We have run the lounge through three monsoons without a closed day.

Can I bring my own bank for the loan?

Yes. Seven banks have approved the project; any of them can lend without a fresh legal check. Others take about three weeks.

What are the monthly maintenance charges?

₹4.20 per sq ft, fixed for the first two years, which covers the club, security, the lifts and the gardens. A sinking fund of ₹60 per sq ft is collected once at possession.

Are there homes for people who work from home?

The 3 BHK with study was drawn for that. The study is 9 by 10 feet with its own window, a door and a wired data point.

Block B,
the H-block

The middle block, every flat a two bedroom of the same plan, 994 or 1,100 sq ft. Phase I, completed 30 June 2018. Resale only.

Block B is the H between the two towers. Its fourteen flats a floor are all the same two bedroom plan, with the drawing room and dining across the front and the two bedrooms of ten by twelve feet behind, so a flat here is chosen by floor and by which way the balconies face: the pool side to the east or the courts to the west. The 2013 brochure gives flats 6 and 13 as 994 sq ft and the other twelve as 1,100 sq ft, the same rooms with more balcony.

Near this block

  1. The play area and the lawns

    The children’s play area with the sand pit sits between this block and the pool, in sight of the balconies on the east side; the lawns and the gazebo are on the other.

  2. The track

    The jogging and cycling track passes both ends of the H, so the morning walk starts at the lobby door.

  3. Stilt parking

    The sanctioned plan puts the cars on the open stilt floor under the block, 52 or 42 of them, with two-wheeler bays beside them and a ramp down to the basement.

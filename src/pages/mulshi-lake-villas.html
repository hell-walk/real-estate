A lake that is not for sale

Mulshi is a reservoir, and the shore belongs to the irrigation department. We lease the 190 m of frontage and keep it as a path; the villas sit 40 m back from the high water line, which is what the rules ask and what the monsoon suggests.

Power comes from the grid with a solar roof on each villa, water from two borewells and the rain tanks under the lawn.

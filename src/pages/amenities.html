What is on
the four acres

The club and the pool at the east end, courts at both ends, a track round the outside, and the things that make a Tuesday easier.

Seventy percent of it is ground

The pool and the club

At the east end of the plan, beside Block A: a swimming pool with a children’s pool next to it, and the clubhouse with a multipurpose hall, a gymnasium and a yoga room. Shared by all three blocks since November 2024.

The courts

A half basketball court, a tennis court and a badminton court, with a skating rink. The plan puts courts at both ends, so Block C has its own set at the west end and Block A has the ones by the club.

The track

A jogging and cycling track round the outside of the three blocks, lit in the evenings, with the landscaped gardens and lawns inside it and a gazebo where the lawns meet.

For children and grandparents

A children’s play area with a sand pit, a senior citizens’ sitting area, and an amphitheatre for the society’s functions. The play area is between Block B and the pool, where it can be seen from the H-block balconies.

Water, power, and the gate

Power backup around the clock, water supply around the clock with rainwater harvesting, a fire fighting system in every block, CCTV, intercom to every flat, and a gated entrance on the south road manned day and night. Lifts in every block.

Everyday things

A convenience store and the society office at the gate, 11 shops of 11 to 15 sq m on the ground floor of Block A, covered parking for every home with basement parking under Blocks B and C, and visitor bays above ground.

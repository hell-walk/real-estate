What is on
the four acres

The club and the pool at the east end, courts at both ends, a track round the outside, and the things that make a Tuesday easier.

Seventy percent of it is ground

The pool and the club

At the east end of the plan, beside Block A: a swimming pool of 102 sq m on the sanctioned plan with a baby pool and a jacuzzi beside it, and the clubhouse: a gymnasium, a yoga hall, a banquet hall, and an indoor games room with snooker, table tennis, air hockey and foosball. Shared by all three blocks since November 2024.

The courts

A lawn tennis court, a basketball court, a badminton court and a squash court, a cricket net for practice, and a skating rink. The plan puts courts at both ends, so Block C has its own set at the west end and Block A has the ones by the club.

The track

A jogging and cycling track round the outside of the three blocks, lit in the evenings, with 1,800 sq m of landscaped garden inside it, a fountain at the gate and a gazebo where the lawns meet.

For children and grandparents

A children’s play area with a sand pit, a senior citizens’ sitting area, and an amphitheatre for the society’s functions. The play area is between Block B and the pool, where it can be seen from the H-block balconies.

Water, power, and the gate

Power backup around the clock, water supply around the clock with rainwater harvesting, a fire fighting system in every block, CCTV, intercom to every flat, and a gated entrance on the south road manned day and night. Lifts in every block.

Everyday things

A convenience store and the society office at the gate, 11 shops of 11 to 15 sq m on the ground floor of Block A, parking on the open stilt floor under every block with ramps down to a basement, and visitor bays inside the gate.

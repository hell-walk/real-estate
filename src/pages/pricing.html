How the money goes, on a resale

  1. Token

    ₹2 L to the owner to hold the flat while the title, the society’s no dues letter and the bank’s legal check are done. Sample plan; the agreement sets the real terms.

  2. Agreement

    10% of the price, less the token, on the agreement to sell. The loan application goes in the same week.

  3. Loan

    The bank pays the owner directly on sanction. Axis, Bank of Baroda, HDFC, ICICI and Punjab National Bank have the project on their approved lists.

  4. Registration

    The balance at the sub-registrar’s office, with stamp duty and registration paid. Then the keys, the society transfer, and the meters in your name.

What the price includes

The flat as built: the builder’s Phase II sheet lists 5 by 2.5 ft Italian design tiles in the living room, dining and bedrooms; non-skid tiles in the kitchen, toilets and balconies; a quartz counter with a tile dado and a steel sink; glazed tiles to 8 ft and Grohe fittings in the toilets; laminated flush doors in 8 ft WPC frames; UPVC windows; concealed copper wiring with modular switches; putty on every inside wall; and 1 KVA of backup a flat. One parking bay on the stilt floor under the block. Membership of the club and the pool comes with the flat.

Not included: whatever the present owner has added on a resale, which is negotiated flat by flat, and the maintenance deposit the society asks of a new member.

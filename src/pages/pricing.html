How the money goes, for an under construction home

  1. Booking

    ₹2 L to hold the home for 21 days while the agreement is drafted. Returned in full if you walk away in that time.

  2. Agreement

    10% of the price less the booking amount, with the registered agreement to sell. Stamp duty is paid here.

  3. By slab

    70% in fourteen calls as each slab of your block is cast, with a photograph and the engineer’s certificate every time. Your bank pays these against the certificate.

  4. At the keys

    The last 20% on the occupation certificate, with the society charges and the sinking fund. Then the keys.

What the price includes

One car bay underground, the club membership for the household, full power backup, piped gas, the modular kitchen with a hob and chimney, wardrobes in every bedroom, a water purifier, and the balcony grilles. Air conditioning points in every room, with the machines left to you.

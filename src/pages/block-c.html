Block C,
the west tower

The X-shaped tower at the west end, with the second set of courts beside it. Phase I, completed 30 June 2018. Resale only.

Block C is the older of the two towers and the mirror of Block A: the 2013 brochure draws the same five plans on the same flat numbers, 1 and 8 the four bedroom with a study, 2, 7, 9, 14, 3 and 6 the three bedroom with a study, 10 and 13 the plain three bedroom, 4, 5, 11 and 12 the two bedroom, at super areas a few per cent smaller than the Phase II figures: 2,450, 1,894, 1,906, 1,769 and 1,294 sq ft. Resale only, with the society’s no dues letter and the owner’s chain of title to check.

Near this block

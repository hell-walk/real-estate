Block C,
the west tower

The X-shaped tower at the west end, with the second set of courts beside it. Phase I, completed 30 June 2018. Resale only.

Block C is the older of the two towers and the mirror of Block A: the same four plans on the same flat numbers, 1 and 8 the four bedroom corners, with a basement for parking under the ground floor. It stands at the quiet end of the plot, furthest from the gate and the shops, with the west courts between it and the boundary.

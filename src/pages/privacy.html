Privacy

Last changed 6 November 2026. This is a sample policy for a sample website.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no cookie set by us. The fonts load from Fontshare and the 3D library from jsDelivr; each of those services sees your IP address when your browser asks for the file, as with any website.

The booking form

The form on the site is checked in your browser and then opens an email on your own device addressed to us, with the details you typed. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email program. Once sent, we keep the email to arrange the visit and delete it ninety days after the visit unless you buy a home, in which case it joins your file.

If you buy

Your agreement, identity documents and payment records are kept for as long as the law asks, which for property is a long time. They are shared with the registrar, your bank, the society once it forms, and nobody else.

Your rights

Write to hello@aranyaestates.example to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have the booking emails deleted earlier. We answer within fourteen days.

Pictures on this site

The photographs are open licence pictures of the Sahyadri country, credited in the footer of every page. They show the region, not our projects.

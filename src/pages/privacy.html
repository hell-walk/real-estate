Privacy

Last changed 6 November 2026. This is a sample policy for an unofficial sample website about Ratan Galaxy.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no cookie set by us. The fonts load from Fontshare and the 3D library from jsDelivr; each of those services sees your IP address when your browser asks for the file, as with any website.

The booking and question forms

What you type in the booking form and the question form is checked in your browser and then saved on the server that runs this site, with the time it arrived. It is used to arrange the visit or answer the question, and deleted ninety days after the visit unless you buy a home, in which case it joins your file. A booking can be looked up and cancelled with its reference and the last four digits of the mobile number.

If you buy

Your agreement, identity documents and payment records are kept for as long as the law asks, which for property is a long time. They are shared with the sub-registrar, your bank, the society, and nobody else.

Your rights

Write to lounge@ratangalaxy.example to see what is held about you, to correct it, or to have a booking deleted earlier. We answer within fourteen days.

Pictures on this site

The photographs are open licence pictures of Lucknow from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the footer of every page. They show the city, not Ratan Galaxy. The plans and the 3D model are drawn from public RERA records and the builder’s published floor plans.

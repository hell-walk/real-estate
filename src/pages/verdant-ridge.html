The stream decides the plan

A monsoon stream cuts the plateau from north to south. We did not pipe it. The six blocks stand in two groups either side of it, the walking loop crosses it twice on basalt steps, and the children’s lawn sits in the bend where it slows.

In the dry months it is a line of boulders and ferns; from June to October it runs. Either way nobody’s window looks at a wall.

A club on the western edge

24,000 sq ft on two floors at the point where the plateau falls away: a 25 m lap pool, a gym that opens onto the lawn, a library with the quiet rule, two squash courts, a crèche, and a terrace built for watching rain arrive over Mulshi.

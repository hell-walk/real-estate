Buying

Is Verdant Ridge RERA registered?

Yes. The project carries a Maharashtra RERA registration; on this sample site the number is a placeholder and the certificate is shown at the lounge, with the approved plans and the title report.

What is the possession date, and what if it slips?

December 2028 for blocks A to D, June 2029 for E and F. The agreement sets a penalty of ₹14 per sq ft a month to you after a six month grace period. We have paid it once, at Pashan Green, and would rather not again.

Can I choose my floor and my block?

Yes, from whatever is unsold on the day. The lounge keeps a live chart. Higher floors cost the same as lower ones; corner homes are the 4 BHK plan and are priced as such.

Can I hold a home while I decide?

₹2 L holds it for 21 days, returned in full if you walk away within that time. After 21 days it becomes part of the booking amount.

Can an NRI buy?

Yes, under the usual FEMA rules, with payment from an NRE or NRO account. We have done the paperwork many times and can do it by post and video.

Will the price go up?

The price you sign is the price you pay. The list price has risen twice since launch and may again; the agreement fixes yours.

Money

Can I bring my own bank for the loan?

Yes. Seven banks have approved the project; any of them can lend without a fresh legal check. Others take about three weeks for their own search, which we help with.

How much GST is there?

5% on the price of an under construction home at Verdant Ridge and the villas, paid with each call. None on the Terraces, which has its occupation certificate.

What are the monthly maintenance charges?

₹4.20 per sq ft at the Ridge, fixed for the first two years, covering the club, security, lifts and gardens. The society takes over after that. A sinking fund of ₹60 per sq ft is collected once at possession and held by the society.

What does the EMI come to?

Depends on the home and the loan. The reader on the pricing page works it out for any home on the list, at today’s rates.

Is there a discount for paying more up front?

A 20:80 plan pays 20% at agreement and 80% at possession through a bank, at a price 2% above the list. Paying the full amount at agreement takes 2% off. Everything in between is the slab plan at the list price.

Living there

How is the ridge road in the monsoon?

Tarred and lit for the full 3.8 km since 2022, with a storm drain on the hill side. We have run the lounge through three monsoons without a closed day. It fogs over in July; drive slowly and enjoy it.

Where does the water come from?

Rain tanks of 2.1 million litres under the lawns, two borewells, and a municipal connection at the gate. The treated water from the sewage plant goes to the gardens, never to the homes.

What about power cuts?

Full backup for homes and common areas from two generators, with a 180 kW solar roof over the parking that carries the daytime common load.

Is there a school bus?

Three schools run buses to the gate; the water tank at the foot of the road is where they turn. The pick up point has a shelter and a guard.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. The loop is a good walk, and the stream is better than any bath.

Are there homes for people who work from home?

The 3 BHK with study was drawn for that. The study is 9 by 10 feet with its own window, a door and a wired data point. Fibre comes to every home from two providers.

Buying

Is Ratan Galaxy RERA registered?

Yes, twice. Phase I, Blocks B and C, is UPRERAPRJ8949. Phase II, Block A, is UPRERAPRJ718664. The promoter, Ratan Housing Development Limited of Kanpur, is UPRERAPRM15031. Both projects are marked complete on the UP RERA register.

Is it ready to move?

Yes, all three blocks. Phase I was completed on 30 June 2018 and Phase II on 13 November 2024. There is no possession date to wait for and no GST on the price.

Am I buying from the builder or from an owner?

Blocks B and C are resale: you buy from the owner, with the society’s no dues letter and the bank’s title check. In Block A some homes and seven of the eleven shops are still with the builder on this sample site; the rest are resale.

Can I choose my floor and my flat?

From what is on the market that week. The lounge keeps a chart. Each floor of Blocks A and C has the same fourteen flats: two 4 BHK at 1 and 8, four of each 3 BHK plan, four 2 BHK. Block B is fourteen 2 BHK a floor. The block pages list them.

Can I see a flat before I visit?

Yes. The 3D viewer opens any flat by block, floor and number, drawn from the published floor plans. It is a model, not a survey; the flat on the day prevails.

Can I hold a home while I decide?

On a resale, a ₹2 L token to the owner holds it while the title is checked; the terms of its return are in the token receipt. Sample plan on this site.

Can an NRI buy?

Yes, under the usual FEMA rules, with payment from an NRE or NRO account. The paperwork can be done by post and video.

Money

Can I bring my own bank for the loan?

Yes. Axis, Bank of Baroda, HDFC, ICICI and Punjab National Bank list the project as approved and can lend without a fresh legal check. Others take about three weeks for their own search, which the desk helps with.

Is there GST?

No. Both phases hold completion certificates, and GST is not charged on a completed home. Stamp duty and registration in Uttar Pradesh come to 7% and 1% of the price.

What are the monthly maintenance charges?

Billed by the society. The sample figure on this site is ₹2.50 a sq ft a month, which covers security, the lifts, the gardens, the club and the pool. The society also asks a transfer fee on a resale, ₹25,000 in the sample.

What does the EMI come to?

Depends on the home and the loan. The reader on the pricing page works it out for any home type on the list, at today’s rates, on the sample prices.

Are the prices on this site real?

No. They are samples at about ₹6,900 a sq ft of super area, the 2025 resale rate quoted on the portals. The owner or the builder quotes the real price for a particular flat.

Living there

Where do I park?

On the open stilt floor under your own block: the sanctioned plan gives Block A 68 car bays and the other two 52 and 42, with two-wheeler bays beside them and ramps down to a basement. Visitor bays are inside the gate.

Where does the water and the power come from?

Water supply around the clock with rainwater harvesting on the ground, and power backup for homes and common areas around the clock. The fire fighting system runs through every block.

How far is Shaheed Path?

Two to three kilometres. Sultanpur Road is about 5.7 km, the airport about 11 km, Charbagh station about 9 km. The location page has the list.

Which schools and hospitals are close?

Lucknow Public School about a kilometre away, Ambedkar University about three. SGPGI and Medanta are three to four kilometres.

Are pets allowed?

The society’s bye-laws decide. The track round the outside is a good walk.

What are the shops in Block A?

Eleven shops of 11 to 15 sq m on the ground floor, facing the sector road. On this sample site seven are still with the builder; the society office and a convenience store are at the gate.

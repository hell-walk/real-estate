Three blocks,
in a row

Two X-shaped towers with an H-shaped block between them, 126 homes each, nine floors of fourteen flats. All three are built and lived in.

The published master plan shows the three blocks side by side along the plot, with the main gate on the south road, the pool and the club at the east end and courts at both ends. The plan does not label the blocks; on this site the west tower is Block C, the middle block is Block B and the east tower, the one with the shops, is Block A, as we read the plan against the two RERA phases.

Two phases, both complete

  1. 2013, April

    Phase I begins. Blocks B and C on 10,110 sq m, 252 homes over stilt parking, on the scheme Space Designers of Gomti Nagar drew in 2012. UPRERAPRJ8949. Architect of record Swati Bhatia.

  2. 2018, June

    Phase I complete on 30 June. The society forms; the office opens at the gate.

  3. 2022, February

    Phase II begins. Block A on 6,741 sq m at the east end, 126 homes and 11 shops. UPRERAPRJ718664.

  4. 2024, November

    Phase II complete on 13 November. The pool and the club open to all three blocks.

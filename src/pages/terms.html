What this site is

A description of homes for sale, written to be accurate on the day it was published. It is not an offer, an invitation to offer, or part of any agreement. The agreement to sell, registered under RERA, is the only document that binds either of us.

Prices and areas

Prices are the list price on the day of publication, before stamp duty, registration and GST, and may change until an agreement is signed. Carpet areas are as per RERA; plans on this site are sample drawings and the approved plans at the lounge prevail.

Driving times and dates

Driving times were measured by us on weekday mornings in October 2026 and will vary. Possession dates are those in the RERA registration; the agreement sets what happens if they slip.

The EMI reader

The reader on the pricing page uses the standard reducing balance formula and the rate you set. It is arithmetic, not a loan offer; a bank decides the rate and the amount.

Testimonials

Quotations on this site are marked as sample notes and are not statements by real buyers.

Linking and copying

Link to any page. The photographs carry their own licences, credited in the footer; the text and drawings are ours.

Law

Indian law, Pune courts, and the Maharashtra RERA authority for anything about a home.

Last changed 6 November 2026. This is an unofficial sample website about Ratan Galaxy. It is not published by and is not affiliated with Ratan Housing Development Limited. The prices, the people, the lounge and the quoted residents are samples.

What this site is

A description of an existing apartment complex, written from public records and listings to be accurate on the day it was published. It is not an offer, an invitation to offer, or part of any agreement. An agreement to sell, registered as the law requires, is the only document that binds a buyer and a seller.

Facts, prices and areas

The building facts come from the UP RERA register and the master plan and floor plans the builder has published. Carpet areas are from the RERA unit lists; super areas and flat numbers from the published floor plans. Prices are samples at about ₹6,900 a square foot, the 2025 resale rate on the portals; the owner or the builder quotes the real price for a particular flat.

Plans and the 3D model

The plans on this site and the flats in the 3D viewer are drawn from the published floor plans and the room dimensions on them. They are illustrations, not surveys. The approved plans and the flat on the day prevail.

Distances and dates

Distances are from the listings and approximate; the minutes beside them are samples. Completion dates are those on the RERA register.

The EMI reader

The reader on the pricing page uses the standard reducing balance formula and the rate you set. It is arithmetic, not a loan offer; a bank decides the rate and the amount.

Testimonials

Quotations on this site are marked as samples and are not statements by real residents.

Linking and copying

Link to any page. The photographs carry their own licences, credited in the footer; the text and drawings are ours.

Law

Indian law, Lucknow courts, and the Uttar Pradesh RERA authority for anything about a home.

Ratan Galaxy,
in plain figures

378 homes and 11 shops in three blocks on 4.16 acres, finished in two phases, 2018 and 2024. Everything on this page is from the public record except the people, who are samples.

Ratan Galaxy stands on plots GH-01 and GH-02 of Sector 12, Vrindavan Yojna, the housing scheme south of Shaheed Path. Blocks B and C went up first, Block A with the shops came six years later. Both phases hold their completion certificates, so what is for sale now is either resale from an owner or one of the last homes with the builder.

Eleven years, in order

  1. 1990s

    Ratan Housing Development Limited is set up in Kanpur. Its promoter registration with UP RERA is UPRERAPRM15031.

  2. 2013, April

    Phase I starts on 10,110 sq m: Blocks B and C, 252 homes, basement parking under both. Architect Swati Bhatia. RERA number UPRERAPRJ8949.

  3. 2018, June

    Phase I completed on 30 June. Families move into B and C; the society office opens at the gate.

  4. 2022, February

    Phase II starts on the 6,741 sq m at the east end: Block A, 126 homes and 11 shops on the ground floor. RERA number UPRERAPRJ718664.

  5. 2024, November

    Phase II completed on 13 November. The pool and the club at the east end open to all three blocks.

  6. 2025

    Resale listings quote about ₹6,900 a square foot of super area. The last homes in Block A and some of the shops are still with the builder.

How it is built

An RCC frame designed for earthquake loading, with lifts in every block. Blocks B and C have a basement for parking under the ground floor; Block A has covered and surface parking at ground level. Every flat has a store room and at least two balconies; the 3 and 4 BHK plans add a servant’s room with its own toilet.

Inside, the builder’s Phase II sheet lists 5 by 2.5 ft Italian design tiles in the living room, dining and bedrooms; non-skid tiles in the kitchen, toilets and balconies; a quartz counter with a tile dado and a steel sink; glazed tiles to 8 ft and Grohe fittings in the toilets; laminated flush doors in 8 ft WPC frames; UPVC windows; concealed copper wiring with modular switches; putty on every inside wall; and 1 KVA of backup a flat. The older listings for Blocks B and C say vitrified tiles, a granite counter and aluminium windows.

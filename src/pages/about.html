Aranya Estates,
Pune, since 2009

Six projects, 370 homes handed over, all of them on time but one, which was four months late and which we still talk about.

Aranya means forest. We started in 2009 with 36 homes in Baner and an office on the ground floor that we never moved out of. We build on hills because flat land in Pune is spoken for, and because a hill gives every home something to look at.

Seventeen years, in order

  1. 2009

    Founded by Meera Kulkarni and Sameer Deshpande with the Baner Court plot and a bank loan the bank still mentions.

  2. 2010

    Baner Court, 36 homes, handed over in August. The office on its ground floor is still ours.

  3. 2013

    Aundh Row, 22 row houses. The first project with dressed basalt from the site; the mason who taught us is now our head of stone.

  4. 2016

    Pashan Green, 120 homes by the lake, planted a year before the plinths. Four months late, our only miss; the penalty was paid in full.

  5. 2019

    Koregaon Court, 48 homes around a courtyard, sold out before plaster.

  6. 2023

    Aranya Terraces, 96 stepped homes above Baner. Occupation certificate in June, families in by the monsoon.

  7. 2025

    Verdant Ridge breaks ground on the ridge road. Mulshi Lake Villas land assembled.

How we build

We keep our own site teams rather than handing the job to a contractor, so the mason who cut the stone for the Terraces is cutting it for the Ridge. Each slab is certified by an outside engineer before a payment call goes out, and the photograph goes to every buyer the same day.

We do not sell what we have not drawn. The sample home is finished before the first agreement, and the plan in the brochure is the plan on the wall.

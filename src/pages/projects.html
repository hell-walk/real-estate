Three projects,
one ridge

Every home we are selling sits on the hills west of Pune: the ridge above Bavdhan, the hill above Baner, and the shore of Mulshi lake.

We buy land that already has a view and then try not to spoil it. Low blocks, most of the ground left open, and nothing built where the next buyer’s window would look.

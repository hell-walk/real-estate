Which flat is which

The plans give 1, 8, 3, 13, 6, 10 and 14; the rest are this site’s reading of the floor plan against the RERA counts, and the viewer uses the same numbering.

What the flats are finished with

As built, the builder’s Phase II sheet lists 5 by 2.5 ft Italian design tiles in the living room, dining and bedrooms; non-skid tiles in the kitchen, toilets and balconies; a quartz counter with a tile dado and a steel sink; glazed tiles to 8 ft and Grohe fittings in the toilets; laminated flush doors in 8 ft WPC frames; UPVC windows; concealed copper wiring with modular switches; putty on every inside wall; and 1 KVA of backup a flat. The older listings for Blocks B and C say vitrified tiles, a granite counter and aluminium windows. On a resale, what the owner added on top is negotiated flat by flat.

The structure is an RCC frame designed for earthquake loading, with lifts in every block and a fire fighting system through each.

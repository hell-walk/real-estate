Block A,
the east tower

The X-shaped tower nearest the pool and the club, with 11 shops along its ground floor. Phase II, completed 13 November 2024. The last homes with the builder are here.

Block A went up second, on the 6,741 sq m at the east end of the plot, and shares its two X-tower plans with Block C: on every floor flats 1 and 8 are the four bedroom corners, four flats are the larger three bedroom with the servant’s room, four the 1,747 sq ft three bedroom, and four the two bedroom. There is no basement; parking is covered at ground level, and the eleven shops face the sector road.

Near this block

  1. The pool and the club

    Right beside the block at the east end: the swimming pool, the children’s pool, the hall, the gym and the yoga room. The club opened with this block in November 2024.

  2. The courts

    The tennis court, the half basketball court and the badminton court by the club. The skating rink is between here and Block B.

  3. The shops and the gate

    Eleven shops along the ground floor facing the road; the main gate on the south road is a short walk, with the society office and the convenience store beside it.

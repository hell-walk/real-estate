Block A,
the east tower

The X-shaped tower nearest the pool and the club, with 11 shops along its ground floor. Phase II, completed 13 November 2024. The last homes with the builder are here.

Block A went up second, on the 6,741 sq m at the east end of the plot, and on the five plans the Phase II brochure draws for an X tower: on every floor flats 1 and 8 are the four bedroom corners with a study, 2, 7, 9 and 14 and 3 and 6 the three bedroom with a study, 10 and 13 the plain three bedroom, and 4, 5, 11 and 12 the two bedroom. The sanctioned plan puts 68 cars on the open stilt floor under the block, with the eleven shops and the community hall at the same level and two-wheeler bays beside them.

Near this block

  1. The pool and the club

    Right beside the block at the east end: the swimming pool, the children’s pool, the hall, the gym and the yoga room. The club opened with this block in November 2024.

  2. The courts

    The tennis court, the half basketball court and the badminton court by the club. The skating rink is between here and Block B.

  3. The shops, the parking and the gate

    Eleven shops along the stilt floor facing the road, with 68 car bays and the two-wheeler bays under the block; the main gate on the south road is a short walk, with the society office and the convenience store beside it.

Where it stands

  1. 2017, August

    Phase II first registered with UP RERA on 12 August as UPRERAPRJ9814, for 37 months, with the prices the builder filed: ₹29.9 L for a 2 BHK, ₹51 L for the 4 BHK.

  2. 2022, February

    Registered again as UPRERAPRJ718664 and work starts on the east plot.

  3. 2024, November

    Completed on 13 November. Keys handed over; the club and the pool open to all three blocks.

  4. 2025

    The last homes and seven of the eleven shops still with the builder, on this sample site. Resale listings at about ₹6,900 a sq ft.
